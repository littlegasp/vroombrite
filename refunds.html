Cancellation & refund policy

Effective 5 November 2026

Rescheduling and cancelling

  • 2 hours or more before your slot: reschedule or cancel for free. Just message us on WhatsApp.
  • Less than 2 hours before, or vehicle not accessible when we arrive: we may charge a visit fee of ₹99.
  • If we have to cancel, for example because of heavy rain or a washer being unavailable, you can choose a free reschedule or a full refund of anything you paid in advance.

Refunds on online payments

  • If you paid online and cancel 2 hours or more before your slot, you get a full refund.
  • Refunds go back to the UPI ID or account you paid from, within 5–7 working days.

Not happy with the result?

Tell us within 24 hours, with a photo if you can. We'll come back and redo the affected area free of charge. If we can't put it right, we'll refund the price of that service.

Monthly plans

  • Cancel any time before your next month starts. There's no lock-in.
  • The current month isn't refunded once it has started, but you can use any remaining washes within that month.
  • If we miss a scheduled wash, we'll add an extra wash or refund that wash, whichever you prefer.

How to ask

WhatsApp or call with your booking ID (it starts with VB-).
