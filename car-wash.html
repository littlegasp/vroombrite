04 — Car wash questions

Good to know.

Is it safe for my paint and coatings?

Yes. We use pH-neutral shampoo, a foam pre-wash and clean microfibre for each car, which is gentle on paint, ceramic coatings and PPF.

Can you wash in a basement or stilt parking?

Yes, as long as there's room to walk around the car. We keep runoff low and clean the spot before leaving.

Do you wash the interior?

Foam & glow and Deep refresh include an interior vacuum and wipe-down. For stains or smells, add an interior deep clean from Detailing.

Which size is my car?

Hatchback: Swift, i20, Baleno, Tiago. Sedan: City, Verna, Dzire, Ciaz. SUV / MUV: Creta, Nexon, Brezza, Innova, XUV700. If unsure, pick the closer one and we'll confirm.
