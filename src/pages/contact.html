Questions

Before you book.

Do I need to give you water or a power point?

No. We carry a water tank and a battery-powered pressure washer and vacuum, so you don't need to arrange anything.

Will my society allow it?

We work neatly inside your parking spot, keep runoff low and clean up before leaving. If your society needs prior intimation, we'll share the washer's name and number in advance.

Do I need to be there?

Not for an exterior wash. For interior cleaning, leave the keys with security or meet us for five minutes at the start.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Message us at least 2 hours before your slot and we'll move it at no charge.
