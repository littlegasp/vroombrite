Privacy policy

Effective 5 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Vroombrite ("we") collects when you use this website or book a doorstep car or bike wash, how we use it and the choices you have. We follow India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

What we collect

  • Booking details: your name, mobile number, building or society address, area and PIN code, vehicle type and number, chosen services, date and time slot, and any notes you add.
  • Messages: what you send us through the contact form or WhatsApp.
  • Payments: if you pay online, your UPI app or payment provider handles the payment. We receive the amount, status and transaction reference. We never see or store your card, bank or UPI PIN details.

How we use it

  • To schedule your wash, send our washer to the right place and contact you about your booking.
  • To keep records of services and payments for accounting and tax.
  • To improve our services and service areas.
  • To send you offers or reminders on WhatsApp only if you agree. Reply STOP at any time to opt out.

Where it's kept and who sees it

Bookings and messages are stored in a Google Sheet (Google) and in WhatsApp (Meta), and only our team can access them. The washer assigned to your booking sees only what they need to do the job: your name, address, slot and vehicle. We do not sell or rent your information. We share it only with the service providers named here or when the law requires us to.

This website

This website has no advertising or analytics trackers. Fonts load from Google Fonts, so your browser connects to Google to fetch them. When you choose a package on one page, the site uses your browser's session storage to carry that choice to the booking form, and it's cleared when you close the tab.

How long we keep it

We keep booking records for as long as we need them to serve you and to meet accounting and tax requirements. You can ask us to delete your information sooner unless we're legally required to keep it.

Your rights

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, correct it, delete it or withdraw your consent to marketing. Message us on WhatsApp or call and we'll respond within 7 days.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
