03 — Detailing questions

Good to know.

How long does a detailing visit take?

Between 45 minutes and 4 hours depending on the treatment. We'll tell you the exact time when we confirm your slot.

Do you need my car to be in a covered parking?

For polishing and sealant, shade is best. Basement, stilt or covered parking all work. Open parking is fine for interior work.

Is a ceramic sealant the same as a ceramic coating?

No. A spray sealant is a lighter, more affordable layer for gloss and water beading. A full ceramic coating is a multi-day workshop job, which we don't offer at your doorstep.
