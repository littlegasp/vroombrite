03 — Plan questions

Good to know.

What if I'm travelling one week?

Tell us a day before and we'll skip that wash. Unused washes can be used later in the same month.

What happens when it rains?

On Always Glossy, we re-wash for free if it rains within 24 hours of an exterior wash. During heavy monsoon days we may move your slot by a day.

Can I add my second car or bike?

Yes. Each vehicle has its own plan, and we wash them in the same visit.

How do I cancel?

Message us before your next month starts. There's no lock-in.
