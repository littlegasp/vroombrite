Terms of service

Effective 5 November 2026

These terms apply when you book a doorstep car or bike wash, detailing or monthly plan with Vroombrite. By booking, you agree to them.

Bookings

  • A booking sent from this website is a request until we confirm it on WhatsApp or by phone.
  • Time slots are arrival windows. Bad traffic or weather can make us a little late, and we'll message you if so.
  • You confirm that you own the vehicle or have the owner's permission, and that washing is allowed where it's parked.

Access to your vehicle

  • The vehicle must be reachable, with enough space to walk around it.
  • For interior services, keys must be with you or your security when we arrive.
  • Please remove valuables before the wash. We're not responsible for items left inside.

Prices and payment

  • Prices are shown on this website. If your vehicle needs much more work than usual, such as very heavy mud, pet hair or stains, we'll tell you the extra cost before starting, and you can say no.
  • Pay after the service by UPI or cash once you've checked the result. This is our standard option.
  • Pay online in advance by UPI when you book, if you prefer.
  • Monthly plans are paid at the start of each month.

Your vehicle's condition

  • Before starting, we look over the vehicle and point out any existing scratches, dents, loose parts or damage.
  • We aren't responsible for damage that already existed, or for parts that are loose, faulty or badly fitted.
  • If we cause damage, tell us within 24 hours with photos. We'll look into it and make it right.

Weather and safety

We may move your slot during heavy rain or when it's unsafe to work. We can also decline a job if the location is unsafe for our washer.
