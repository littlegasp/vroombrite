04 — Bike wash questions

Good to know.

Will water get into the electricals?

We cover the ignition, switchgear and exhaust before foaming and use low pressure around electrical parts.

Do you wash electric scooters?

Yes, with a damp-wash method around the battery and charging port, never a direct spray.

Can you wash several bikes in one society?

Yes. Book each bike, or message us on WhatsApp for a group slot in the same parking.
